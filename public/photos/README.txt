Photo dump albums
====================

Each subfolder here becomes its own section on /photo-dump.

Examples:
  public/photos/travel/IMG_001.jpg
  public/photos/lab-notes/plate-scan.png
  public/photos/misc.jpg          → appears under "Misc" (root-level files)

Supported: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .gif .avif

Click any image to open the fullscreen viewer (arrow keys to browse within an album).
