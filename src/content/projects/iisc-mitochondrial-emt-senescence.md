## Overview

Ten-month **Project Intern** at the Indian Institute of Science (Saini Lab & Mathematical Oncology Lab): *Mitochondrial Control of State Transitions — Convergent Mechanisms in EMT and Senescence.*

## Computational

- Automated **R pipelines** for bulk and single-cell RNA-seq across **15 GEO datasets** — cut preprocessing time by ~**60%**
- Meta-analysis integrating TCGA, NCI-60, and GEO to find conserved signatures tying mitochondrial stress to senescence
- **ODE-based signaling network models** in Python to predict dynamic responses to radiation in lung adenocarcinoma contexts

## Experimental validation

- Mammalian cell culture (A549, BEAS-2B)
- Flow cytometry and immunofluorescence to confirm in-silico predictions
- Protein quantification (Bradford, BCA), Western blot, SDS-PAGE

## Takeaway

A full translational arc: from messy multi-cohort omics → quantitative models → bench validation — the kind of workflow I want to keep doing at the engineering–biology interface.
