## Overview

NanoMaps is a tracking and navigation system for nanobots moving through the bloodstream, aimed at precision drug delivery. Our team designed the concept at Columbia University’s Healthcare Hackathon 2026 and won the **Advanced Therapeutics** track.

## What I contributed

- Framed the clinical problem: how to localize therapeutic agents without losing spatial context in circulation
- Helped design a real-time tracking layer and navigation logic for simulated nanobot paths
- Pitched the system with an emphasis on translational feasibility and safety constraints

## Outcome

**Winner, Advanced Therapeutics Track** — Columbia Healthcare Hackathon, 2026.
